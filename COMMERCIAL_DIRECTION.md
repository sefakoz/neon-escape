# NEON ESCAPE ∞ — Commercial Art & Production Direction

Status: approved direction, NOT a finished game.

## Product
Premium-looking, colorful, readable sci-fi hybrid-casual survival game. Primary priority: excellent feel and retention, not premature monetization.

## Visual standard
- Cohesive custom or properly licensed sprites with a shared perspective and palette.
- Player: instantly identifiable silhouette, 8-direction movement or directionally consistent animation, idle/hit/death states.
- Enemies: three distinct silhouettes, attack tells, animated movement and hit reactions.
- Arena: visually rich but low-noise background; no obstructive floor shapes, no dominant perspective grid.
- VFX: impact, pickup, damage, shield, explosion, particles with controlled screen shake.
- UI: accessible mobile contrast and safe areas, translucent floating joystick, readable score and life indicators.

## Sound & feel
- Licensed professionally produced audio for hits, explosions, pickups and ambient layers.
- Sound volume controls and reduced-effects option.
- Real iPhone haptics require native iOS integration; browser vibration is not a supported substitute.

## Revenue design (do not enable until gameplay tested)
- Free-to-play.
- Opt-in rewarded ads for a second chance or bonus, never forced in the first session.
- Cosmetic character skins and trails, no pay-to-win.
- Consider season pass only after retention and repeat engagement are demonstrated.

## Production gates
1. Verify the mobile controls and collisions on a real iPhone.
2. Select and license one consistent art pack; record attribution and license.
3. Integrate animations, enemies and environmental art into Godot rebuild.
4. Add licensed SFX and performance tuning.
5. Export a playable mobile/web build and conduct real device QA.
6. Run small player tests and measure tutorial completion, D1/D7 retention, session length, crash-free sessions.
7. Add monetization only after engagement meets target thresholds.

## Honest build status
The GitHub Pages play-v2.html remains a browser Canvas prototype. Godot rebuild source is separate and is not yet a deployed, device-tested game.
